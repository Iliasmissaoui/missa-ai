Lead finding and cold email system

Three workflows that run every weekday. They find the right firms, study each one, write to them personally, and sort every reply so the owner only spends time on interested people.

  • 08:00: searches Google Maps for target firms and skips any already contacted.
  • An AI qualifier reads each website, scores the fit from 1 to 10, and finds a verified email.
  • 09:00: an AI analyst finds the firm's most costly gap and writes an email under 200 words.
  • Every 5 minutes: replies are sorted into Hot, Warm, Cold, Not interested, Unsubscribe or Out of office, with a suggested answer for each.
  • Built-in rules: a daily send limit, an easy opt-out in every email, and permanent do-not-contact for unsubscribes.

AI lead qualifier

A branded web form sends each new lead to an AI that scores it HOT, WARM or COLD, logs it to a CRM sheet, and sends a reply written for that lead's situation.

  • Scores every lead the moment it arrives.
  • Writes a personal reply instead of a generic auto-response.
  • Keeps a clean record of every lead and its score.

Patient assistant in Cameroonian Pidgin

Most AI tools work best in English. Many patients don't. We built a knowledge assistant that understands questions in Cameroonian Pidgin and answers from approved medical information. It was delivered with a full user guide, written the way an AI specialist would hand a system over to a company.

  • Retrieval-based, so it answers only from approved information.
  • Designed for a hospital's patient questions: appointments, preparation and common concerns.
  • Shows we can serve customers in the languages they actually speak.
