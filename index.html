Questions

What people ask us first.

Will AI replace my staff?

No. Our systems take the repetitive work off your team, such as answering the same questions and chasing leads, so your people spend their time on clients and work that earns money.

What happens to my data?

We collect only what the system needs, follow UK GDPR, and can host the automation on a server in the UK or EU so you know where your data lives. We agree the rules with you before anything goes live.

What if the AI gives a wrong answer?

Assistants answer only from information you approve, and hand over to a person for anything they can't answer. During the pilot we check its replies and fix mistakes quickly.

Which languages can you work in?

English, French, Arabic and Spanish, and we have built an assistant in Cameroonian Pidgin. Ask us about others.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what we find in the study. Most clients start with a 30-day pilot for one system at a fixed monthly fee, with no long contract. The first review is free.

Do I need new software?

Usually not. We connect to the tools you already use, such as Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, WhatsApp and your website.
